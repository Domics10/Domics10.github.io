About

I attended the Polytechnic University of Marche, where I worked on data analysis and deep learning projects, in the field of Time Series Analysis.

Projects. Public repositories on GitHub.

Real-SR · Ultrasound

Super-resolution of fetal ultrasound images (HC18 dataset) by adapting Real-SR: degradation kernel estimation with KernelGAN, noise injection and fine-tuning of an ESRGAN/RRDB ×4 network.

TSDecoder

A decoder-only Transformer built from scratch for time series forecasting: masked self-attention, training, checkpoints and forecast plots. Runs in Docker.

Google Play Store Analysis

Analysis of the Google Play Store dataset: cleaning, visualization, regression, classification, clustering and autoregression. University project, with PDF report.
